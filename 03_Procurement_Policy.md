# Fictional Procurement Policy - Training Case

**Business:** Nova Retail Distribution Pvt. Ltd. (fictional); **Request:** PR-NOVA-001

This is a made-up learning policy. It is NOT a real corporate or legal policy.

## Mandatory checks
1. Purchase 20 new wired handheld 2D barcode scanners with USB connectivity.
2. Quote 20 units; no substitute 1D-only or wireless-only models.
3. Full landed cost (unit price * quantity + shipping + demonstration GST) must not exceed INR 145,000.
4. Lead time must not exceed 15 calendar days after receipt of approved purchase order.
5. Warranty must be at least 12 months.
6. Terms and supporting notes must be disclosed, including advance-payment requests.
7. Information not provided by a vendor must be marked `MISSING`, never invented.
8. All approvals are explicitly made by the procurement officer. The assistant must never send a PO, place an order, or authorize a payment.

## Scoring methodology (eligible quotations only)
- Landed cost: 40%, score = (lowest eligible landed cost / vendor landed cost) * 100.
- Warranty: 25%, score = MIN(vendor warranty / 24, 1) * 100.
- Delivery: 20%, score = (fastest eligible lead time / vendor lead time) * 100.
- Support: 15%, score = vendor's fictional support assessment out of 10 * 10.

`Weighted score = price*0.40 + warranty*0.25 + delivery*0.20 + support*0.15`.
The score is a decision aid, not evidence of actual supplier quality.
Support ratings are fictional assessment inputs, not independently verified customer ratings.

## Human approval and override
- System proposes the highest scoring **eligible** supplier.
- Reviewer may choose another eligible supplier, but must record a reason.
- Approvals require an approver name and are audit-logged in the demo sheet.
- A quotation with an unmet mandatory requirement requires separate exception review; this beginner demo cannot approve such exceptions.
- **No real transaction:** Even when approval is marked `Approved`, the capstone only records a *simulated* decision and issues no financial commitments.

## Caveats
GST is assumed at 18% of (unit price * quantity + shipping) purely for practice.
Actual tax rates, freight-taxability, ITC, supplier compliance, and product specifications must be independently checked for real transactions.
